##### CANADA NAVAL NAME LISTS (ISNE) #####
# Note: Vanilla HoI4 CAN_ship_names.txt had these anomalies:
# 1. Battleships and battlecruisers shared one 11-name list; heavy cruisers copied the light cruisers (plus the typo "Kingson").
# 2. The nine provinces sat in light cruisers, heavy cruisers, battleships, battlecruisers and the provinces pool at once.
# 3. Foreign vessels: three Australian destroyers (Vendetta, Voyager, Waterhen), the Royal Navy-only Lancaster, and the Royal Navy submarines Olympus and Osiris.
# 4. River-class frigates listed as destroyers; "Ottawa" twice; "Chaudiere", "St. Catherine's", "St. John" and "Trois-Rivieres" misspelt; "Ile de Québec" untraceable.
# 5. Anachronisms: Nunavut (1999) and post-war suburbs (Mississauga, Laval, Vaughan, Markham, Brampton, Surrey, Burnaby, Richmond, Gatineau).
