##### CHILE NAVAL NAME LISTS (ISNE) #####
# Note: Vanilla HoI4 CHL_ship_names.txt suffered from severe anomalies:
# 1. Non-combat vessels assigned to dreadnoughts and cruisers, including an 1875 Scottish-built
#    4-masted cargo sailing ship converted into a coal pontoon hulk in Punta Arenas ("County of Peebles")
#    placed in CA, BB, and BC, and an 1848 paddle steamer transport whose 1856 peacetime shipwreck
#    claimed 300+ lives in Chile's worst maritime catastrophe ("Cazador") assigned as a battleship.
# 2. Captured 1830s merchantmen, schooners, and auxiliary steamers dumped into frontline fleets
#    ("Lima", "Peruana", "Trujillana", "Betelgeuse", "Majestic", "Rímac", "Rambler", "Gipsy",
#    "Thalaba", "Joven Daniel", "Fortunata", "María", "Begoña", "Jerezana", "Dolores", "Socabaya", etc.).
# 3. Duplicate entries ("Orella" twice in DD, "Araucano" twice in CA, "Valparaiso" and "Valparaíso" in CL).
# 4. Ironclads ("Blanco Encalada", "Cochrane") and pre-dreadnought "Capitán Prat" assigned to destroyers.
# 5. A 20-entry copy-paste tail from DD into SS putting battleships, destroyers, and nouns ("Fósforo") into submarines.
# 6. Corrupted characters/diacritics and backticks used instead of apostrophes ("Capitán O`Brien", "O`Higgins").
# 7. Severe capital ship depletion (only 6 carriers and 1 real battleship).
