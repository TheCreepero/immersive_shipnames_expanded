##### CUBA NAVAL NAME LISTS (ISNE) #####
# Note: Vanilla HoI4 CUB_ship_names.txt suffered from severe anomalies:
# 1) Wrong prefix "ARC " (Armada de la República de Colombia) applied to Cuba;
#    the historical pre-1959 Cuban Navy (Marina de Guerra) did not use an English-style prefix.
# 2) Typographical error "Marinao" instead of "Marianao".
# 3) Complete verbatim duplication between DD and SS lists (identical 9 names).
# 4) Complete verbatim duplication between CL and CA lists (identical 5 names).
# 5) Mirrored 2-ship stubs between BB and BC featuring US President "Theodore Roosevelt".
# 6) Zero universal thematic pools for the Ship Designer.
# ISNE resolves all errors, removes foreign names, segregates classes doctrinally,
# and expands naval flavor across historical and alternate-history fleets.
