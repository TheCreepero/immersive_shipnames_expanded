##### UNITED KINGDOM NAVAL NAME LISTS (ISNE) #####
# Two sets, as in vanilla: the Royal Navy lists (prefix HMS) for every government except communism, and the
# workers' navy lists (prefix WLNS) for a communist Britain, which mirror the HMS lists without royal names.
# ENG_DE, ENG_CLAA, ENG_CVL, ENG_BM and ENG_MINELAYERS_HISTORICAL keep their vanilla tags as universal role pools:
# vanilla history assigns them, with the seven hull groups, to the starting variants.
# Within each set the CL, CA, BB, BC and CV lists share no name, and no role pool shares a name with them.
