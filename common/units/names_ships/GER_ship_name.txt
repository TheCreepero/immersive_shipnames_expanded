##### GERMANY NAVAL NAME LISTS (ISNE) #####
# The file carries vanilla's own file name, GER_ship_name.txt (singular), so that it replaces the vanilla file.
# Two sets, as in vanilla. The Reichsmarine and Kriegsmarine lists carry no prefix and are open to every
# government, so no change of government renames the fleet. The Kaiserliche Marine lists (prefix SMS, submarines SM)
# are open to non-aligned and democratic Germany, the two governments under which the Kaiser can reign; every role
# and thematic list has an SMS copy, and the seven hull lists of that set carry the Imperial rosters.
# Destroyers from Z23, submarines, torpedo boats, minesweepers and fleet escorts are numbered, as the German navies
# numbered them. The ordered entries keep vanilla's numbers, because the 1939 start names its ships by them
# (destroyers 1 to 21, torpedo boats 201 to 211, submarines 1 to 61), and the fallback continues after the last one.
# Names of ships the game starts with stand last in their lists, as in vanilla.
# GER_PZ, GER_TB, GER_MS, GER_CS, GER_CVL and GER_MR_HISTORICAL keep their vanilla tags as universal role pools;
# vanilla history and a decision assign GER_PZ, GER_TB and GER_MR with the seven hull groups.
# Within each set the CL, CA, BB, BC and CV lists share no name, and no role pool shares a name with them.

##### REICHSMARINE AND KRIEGSMARINE (no prefix): every government #####
