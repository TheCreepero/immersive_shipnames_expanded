##### PHILIPPINES NAVAL NAME LISTS (ISNE) #####
# Note: Vanilla HoI4 PHI_ship_names.txt suffered from severe anomalies:
# 1) Critical Submarine Anomaly: Base-game PHI_SS_HISTORICAL contained 18 names—all
#    belonging to Royal New Zealand Navy (Hawea, Pukaki, Taupo, Otago) and Royal Australian
#    Navy (Echuca, Kiama, Inverell) vessels. Zero Philippine names were present.
# 2) "General Manchatas" Mystery: A garbled transcription/OCR artifact present in capital
#    ship lists, completely nonexistent in Philippine military history or NHCP archives.
# 3) Extreme Cross-Hull Duplication: PHI_CA_HISTORICAL was a 100% duplicate of PHI_CL_HISTORICAL,
#    and PHI_BB_HISTORICAL, PHI_BC_HISTORICAL, and PHI_CV_HISTORICAL were 100% duplicate stubs
#    of each other, with cities (Manila, Cabanatuan, Batangas, Cadiz, Davao) and Luzon
#    duplicated across all five classes.
# 4) "Pilipinas Republic": A crude hybrid calque, replaced by authentic foundational designations.
# 5) Shallow destroyer pool (13 names) mixing datus, revolutionary generals, and provinces.
# 6) Zero universal thematic pools for the Ship Designer.
# ISNE resolves all errors, purges foreign vessels, decouples classes doctrinally,
# and equips the Philippines with expansive historical and alternate-history fleet rosters.
