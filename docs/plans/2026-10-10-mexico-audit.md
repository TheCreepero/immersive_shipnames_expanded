## Rationale
- **New `MEX_BC_HISTORICAL` (49)**, BC doctrine per `CLAUDE.md` §2; `battle_cruiser` removed from BB, whose roster (historical macro-regions and the states) is unchanged. Four sections:
  - Coastal fortresses & citadels: San Juan de Ulúa, San Carlos de Perote, Fuerte de San Diego (Acapulco), Baluarte de Santiago (Veracruz), Fuerte de San Miguel (Campeche), Fuerte de Loreto (Puebla), Fuerte de San Felipe (Bacalar), La Ciudadela (Mexico City), and the insurgent forts Fuerte del Sombrero (1817) and Cóporo (1815).
  - Decisive battles & sieges, Independence to Revolution, by bare place name as navies do (*Cuautla*, not *Sitio de Cuautla*). Heroic defences of 1847 (Churubusco, Molino del Rey, Chapultepec, Padierna) are kept as commemorated in Mexico; the rout of Cerro Gordo and the captured squadron of Antón Lizardo were left out. Mariel (1828) is the navy's own last stand of the brig *Guerrero*.
  - Pre-Columbian sea kingdoms & strongholds: Mayapán, Chichén Itzá, Tzintzuntzan, Cempoala, Tututepec, and the Chontal Maya trading polities Xicalango, Acalan and Potonchán. Tulum, Cozumel and Champotón were not used (CL).
  - Historic warships: schooner *Iguala* (1822, first ship of the navy), steam frigate *Guadalupe* (1842), ship of the line *Congreso Mexicano* (ex-*Asia*, 1825), brig *Hermón* (Mariel squadron, 1828), gunboat *Demócrata* (1870s). *Moctezuma*, *Guerrero* and *Bravo* were not used (CA/BB).
- **`can_use` gates**: SOCIALISM `has_government = communism`; NATIONALISM `has_government = fascism`; REPUBLICAN_IDEALS `OR = { democratic neutrality }`, because vanilla Mexico starts non-aligned under the PNR, a republic, and a `democratic`-only gate would lock the pool at game start.
- **`MEX_ADMIRALS`** (tag kept; display name "Admirals" → "Naval Heroes"): 9 removed (1 army general, 8 untraceable), 8 added, `David Coello` → `David Coello Ochoa`.
- **Respellings** (removed + added in the table): DD `Juan José de los Reyes` → `El Pípila` (Juan José de los Reyes Martínez Amaro, known only by the nickname), `Gutiérrez Zamora` → `Manuel Gutiérrez Zamora`, `Pino Suárez` → `José María Pino Suárez`; CA `Pancho Villa` → `Francisco Villa` (the form used officially); MYTHOLOGY `Ometeotl` → `Ometéotl`.
- **CA sections**: Iturbide and Maximiliano moved to a new "Mexican emperors" header, Santa Anna to "Presidents & revolutionary leaders", "Pre-Columbian emperors" renamed "Pre-Columbian rulers". No CA name added or dropped.
- **SAINTS**: `San Juan de Ulúa` (fortress, now in BC) → `San Felipe de Jesús` (Mexican-born saint, patron of Mexico City); `San José del Cabo` (town) → `San José`.
