## Rationale
- **BC (new group, 44 names)**: the KJRM had no capital ships, so the roster follows §2 BC doctrine in four sections: medieval maritime realms (Duklja, Zahumlje, Travunija, Paganija, the Dubrovnik and Poljica republics), historic war-vessel types plus the Hvar and Kotor galleys at Lepanto, fortresses, and straits / channels / naval encounters. `battle_cruiser` was removed from BB `ship_types`. Fallback `"Bojni krstaš %d"`. Kalemegdan, Petrovaradin and Golubac are river fortresses, so the section is headed "Coastal & river fortresses and citadels".
- **BB**: *Dalmacija* -> *Kranjska* (Carniola, a historic land in the same section). *Dalmacija* stays in CL as the real KJRM light cruiser (ex-*Niobe*).
- **CA**: *Kosovo* -> *Kosovo Polje* (the 1389 battlefield); BB keeps the region *Kosovo*.
- **DD**: +*Orjen*, *Suvobor*, *Rudnik*, *Labud* (98 -> 102); respelled *Rasa* -> *Raša*, *Golesnica* -> *Golešnica*.
- **SS**: *Kit* -> *Kit Perajar* (English homonym).
- **CL, CITIES**: *Bar* -> *Stari Bar* (English homonym; the fortified old town). *Stari Bar* was therefore kept out of the BC fortress section.
- **MYTHOLOGY**: *Rod* -> *Mokoš* (English homonym with no natural qualified form; Mokoš is a major goddess with South Slavic toponyms such as Mokošica).
- **RIVERS**: *Rasa* -> *Raša*; display name "Rivers & Waterways" -> "Rivers & Lakes".
- **HEROES**: *Ban Jelačić* moved to the Croatian section (no roster change).
- **CV, BIRDS**: *Sup* -> *Bradan* (English homonym, found in review; the bearded vulture, with *Bjeloglavi Sup* already covering the griffon).
- **RULERS**: display name "Rulers & Dynasties" -> "Rulers" (found in review).
