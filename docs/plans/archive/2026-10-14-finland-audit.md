## Rationale
- **One entry per person or being in a group.** This applies the `CrossClassPerson` logic inside a group:
  - Lemminkäinen kept; Kaukomieli and Ahti Saarelainen are his Kalevala epithets (CA, DD, MYTHOLOGY).
  - Louhi kept; Pohjolan Emäntä is her title (CA).
  - Sampsa (CA) and Sampsa Pellervoinen (MYTHOLOGY) kept; Pellervo is the short form of the same name.
  - Iku-Turso, the historic submarine name, kept; Meritursas and Turso are alternate names of the same being (SS).
- **RULERS.**
  - Mannerheim and Svinhufvud keep only their Presidentti forms, which complete the presidential roster.
  - Presidentti Paasikivi (President 1946–56) completes that roster to the game era.
  - Kuningas Fredrik I (King of Sweden 1720–51) fills the gap between Ulriika Eleonoora and Aadolf Fredrik and holds the count at 36.
- **CA Sammas.** Sammas is an alternate name of the Sampo, which BC holds as the documented icebreaker name *Sampo*. That is one artefact in two forms across the exclusivity set, so CA loses it. MYTHOLOGY keeps it, since thematic pools are outside the exclusivity set.
- **DD.**
  - Tursa is a duplicate or typo of the confirmed patrol vessel Tursas.
  - Nine names sat under "historic" section headers, but no Finnish Navy or Coast Guard vessel was found for them: Huuha, Törppö, Kalla, Kisko, Kiuru, Kuusiluoto, Haapasaari, Vahti, Salpa. Huuha also reads as *huuhaa* ("nonsense").
  - DD has spare depth (120 against a target of 100), so these are dropped rather than kept on an unverified historical claim.
- **BB:** Pohjolanmaa is an unattested coinage. 41 remain against a target of 30.
- **PROVINCES:** Äänisenranta is the modern Prionezhsky district, an anachronism for the game era, so Karjalan kannas (the Karelian Isthmus) replaces it.
- **BEASTS.**
  - Kalliosopuli is not a Finnish species name. The intended wood lemming is Metsäsopuli; this respelling appears as removed + added in the table.
  - Kouvo is unverified as a bear name, so Naali (the Arctic fox) replaces it.
- **SS / FISH.**
  - Kivinuljaska is not on the Finnish fish list.
  - Meritursas is a mythic sea giant, so it leaves FISH: the display name "Aquatic Life & Fish" has to describe the list.
- **Review fixes.**
  - PROVINCES: Karjalan kannas is respelled as the standard compound Karjalankannas.
  - NATURE: Auvo is the poetic word for "bliss", not a weather term, so it is dropped.
  - RIVERS: Lankoski (listed under "Major rivers") gave no hit for a river or rapids, so it is dropped.

## Kept on judgment
- **`CrossClassPerson` CL/BC Vaasa ~ Kustaa Vaasa:** the city is named after the royal house. A place named after a person stays.
- **`Prefix` INFO:** FIN is prefix-free, matching vanilla (`VanillaPrefix` did not fire).
- **Turisas (DD, CA) beside Iku-Turso (DD):**
  - Agricola lists Turisas as the giver of victory in war.
  - Ganander and Lencqvist keep him separate from the sea giant.
  - Equating the two is a disputed scholarly hypothesis.
- **Place synonyms in BB and PROVINCES** (Viena / Vienan Karjala, Aunus / Aunuksen Karjala): these are places, not persons or beings. Aunus also names the town and governorate of Olonets. Kept as in the 2026-09-28 audit.
- **Overlaps already accepted in the 2026-09-28 review:**
  - DD shares Kalevala figures with CA, and words such as Sisu, Tarmo, Voima, Otso, Kontio, Kokko and Korppi with BC and CV; DD is outside the exclusivity set.
  - Species and constellation synonyms: Sääksi / Kalasääski (CV, BIRDS) and Otava / Otavainen (CV).
- **Ideology pools:** FIN has none and `ThemeCount` did not fire. None were added in this delta audit.
- **Bear and wolf names in BEASTS** (Karhu, Kontio, Otso, Mesikämmen, Ohto; Susi, Hukka): the same-being rule covers named persons and mythic individuals, not species. The Finnish taboo names for the bear are a deliberate cultural theme.
- **Koskelo / Isokoskelo (BIRDS):** genus and species, accepted like the other species synonyms. Muikku and Kuore are different species (vendace and smelt).
- **Water spirits in SS** (Vetehinen, Vesihiisi, Näkki, Vedenhaltija):
  - Vetehinen and Vesihiisi are historic submarine names (the Vetehinen class of 1930).
  - Näkki and vedenhaltija are folk categories of water spirit, not a single named being.
- **Ahto (SS) and Ahti (DD, MYTHOLOGY):** the same sea god, but never twice in one group. Ahti Saarelainen was removed from CA because Lemminkäinen sits in the same group.
- **Kave (DD, CA):** an ancient deity of Finnish runic poetry, covered by the mythic-beings entry.
- **NATURE display name "Nature & Tempests":** it covers weather, waves, fire and sky phenomena. "Weather & Sky" would leave out the waves and fire, so the name stays.
