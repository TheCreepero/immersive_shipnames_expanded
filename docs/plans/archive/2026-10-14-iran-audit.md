## Rationale
- English homonyms (§3): `Mad` → `Mada` (Old Persian *Māda*, Behistun form, parallel to `Parthav`); `Dad` → `Dadgari` (justice); `Bum` → `Shahbuf` (Eurasian eagle-owl); `Fok` → `Fok-e Khazari` (Caspian seal, Iran's endemic seal).
- `Kahnamoie` → `Kahnamuie`: spelling used by Wikipedia and navypedia for the Bayandor-class corvette (F28).
- DD headers: Bayandor class reworded to "naval officers killed in 1941" (Naghdi namesake not confirmed); Harischi and Riazi moved to a new "Cape-class inshore minesweepers (1964)" section; "1930s gunboats & minesweepers" renamed "Interwar patrol craft & gunboats" so it covers Shahin (patrol craft 1923–1941) alongside Shahbaz, Karkas and Shahrokh.
- DD depth (96 → 101): Shahnameh heroes Gorgin (son of Milad) and Siamak (son of Kiumars) extend the Saam-class formula; classical arms Pishqabz (Persian dagger), Chomaq (club/mace) and Navak (arrow guide / short arrow) extend the Kaman-class formula.
- SS depth (52 → 60): Gulf and Caspian fish under the existing food-fish section: Halva Siyah, Shurideh, Kutar, Sangsar, Esbeleh (researcher-verified), Sobeyti, Sorkhu, Suf (standard Persian fisheries vocabulary for sobaity seabream, snapper and zander; common nouns, not persons).
- RULERS (31 → 36): Toghrol and Soltan Sanjar (Seljuks), Vushmgir (Ziyarid), Mo'ezz od-Dowleh (Buyid), Shah Mohammad Khodabandeh (Safavid).
- PROVINCES (31 → 36): Ardalan, Mokrian, Ruyan (historic regions); Asurestan, Mishan (Sasanian provinces).
- VIRTUES (33 → 36): Fadakari (self-sacrifice), Azadegi (freedom/nobility), Ghorur (pride).
- BEASTS (28 → 36): Gorbeh-ye Vahshi (wild cat), Gurkan (badger), Rasu (weasel), Sag-e Abi (otter), Shuka (roe deer), Tashi (porcupine), Kharposht (hedgehog), Bozmajeh (desert monitor). Babr and Palang were avoided because DD uses them.
- No cross-class collisions before or after; the BB (sovereigns) / BC (seas, islands, fortresses, battles) split already follows §2.

## Persons verified
- DD ships named after persons: Bayandor (Gholamali Bayandor, killed 25 Aug 1941; Wikipedia); Naghdi, Milanian, Kahnamuie (Bayandor-class corvettes PF-104–106; Wikipedia "Bayandor-class corvette"); Harischi, Riazi (Cape-class MSI-13/14, delivered 1964; GlobalSecurity, navypedia); Artemiz (ex-HMS *Sluys*, named after Artemisia I of Caria; well documented).
- DD admirals: Rasaei (Farajollah Rasaei, IIN commander 1960–1972; Wikipedia); Habibollahi (Kamal Habibollahi, last IIN commander; well documented); Latif Khan (Mohammad Latif Khan, Nader Shah's Gulf admiral; Iranica "Navy i"); Ariabignes, Megabaz (Megabazus son of Megabates), Prexaspes (Herodotus 7.97, Xerxes' fleet admirals); Megabat (Naxos 499 BC), Datis, Artafern (Marathon fleet), Farnabaz (Knidos 394 BC), Tiribaz (Cypriot War fleet), Vahrez (Sasanian sea expedition to Yemen): well documented.
- DD Shahnameh heroes (Saam, Zaal, Rostam, Faramarz, Sohrab … Arash, Gorgin, Siamak): well documented (Ferdowsi, *Shahnameh*).
