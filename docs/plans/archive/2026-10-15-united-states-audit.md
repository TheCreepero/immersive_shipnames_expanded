## Research
- No researcher dispatch. All additions are US cities, battles, pre-1936 presidents and statesmen, and named warships or forts that are well documented; the plausible-expansion names are verifiable as places, not as ships.
- Web checks (2 calls). DD-425 *Madison*: DANFS "Madison III (DD-425)" and Wikipedia name it for Cdr. James J. Madison (1888-1922, Medal of Honor, USS *Ticonderoga* 1918), not the president. *Dallas* as a canceled heavy cruiser: DANFS, "Dallas (CA-150)": Des Moines-class hull canceled 28 March 1945 before keel-laying, number then changed to CA-140, which Wikipedia ("USS Dallas") and Navypedia record as canceled in June 1946 while building.
- Collision screen: a scratch script checked every candidate against all 41 groups (exact names and substrings). It dropped *Austin* and *Lansing* (destroyer-escort names, a role-pool FAIL against CA), *Madison* and *Stockton* (DD person namesakes) and *Lawrence* (DD) and swapped *New Georgia* (reads as a variant of BB *Georgia*) for *Morotai*. Cities already used by the frigates, gunboats, monitors, tenders, cutters or minelayers (*Tacoma*, *Long Beach*, *Grand Rapids*, *Sacramento*, *Camden*, *Passaic*, *Montgomery*, *Tampa*, ...) were never drafted.

## Rationale
Additions only; no legacy entry was removed, renamed or moved, and no group tag changed.
- **CL, CA, AA cruisers (cities):** under the 1819 naming act and the interwar practice, cruisers carry city names. CL grew by 162 cities without a cruiser namesake, in regional sections (New England, Mid-Atlantic, South, Midwest, West, Alaska/Hawaii/island territories), drawn from 1940-census cities and naval towns in every state. CA took 39 larger cities (most above ~50,000 in 1940) plus *Dallas* among the canceled hulls. The AA cruisers took 16 western cities in the Atlanta/Juneau manner, which clears the depth WARN. Left out: anything used by another hull or role pool, cities that read as foreign places or persons (*Athens*, *Rome*, *Moscow*, *Bismarck*, *Pierre*, *Troy*, *Lynn*, *Elizabeth*, *Hamilton*), English words (*Meridian*, *Temple*, *Auburn*, *Laurel*, *Superior*), *Royal Oak* (HMS) and prefixed variants of names already used (*Niagara Falls*, *East St. Louis*, *Michigan City*, *Petersburg*).
- **BB (statesmen):** all 48 states were already in use. Following the BB doctrine (`CLAUDE.md` §2: founding fathers, presidents) and the precedent of the "41 for Freedom" ballistic-missile submarines (the post-war capital ships, named for statesmen), BB gained 20 presidents and founding statesmen, all dead before 1936. Persons already carried by another hull were not used (*George Washington*, *Abraham Lincoln* and both Roosevelts in CV; *Benjamin Franklin* and *John Hancock* through CV *Franklin* and *Hancock*; *Peyton Randolph* through CV *Randolph*). Also skipped: *Alexander Hamilton* and *Andrew Jackson*, whom the Coast Guard cutter names already honour.
- **BC (doctrine: historic warships, fortresses, decisive naval actions):** the sail-frigate *Sabine* and the Civil War steam sloops and ironclad of Farragut's and Du Pont's squadrons joined the existing steam-frigate section; new sections hold three decisive naval actions (*Flamborough Head* 1779, *Port Royal* 1861, *Mobile Bay* 1864) and nine seacoast forts from Boston to Manila Bay. Forts named for a president who now sits in BB (*Fort Monroe*, *Fort Jefferson*, *Fort Adams*), *Fort Mills* (honours the namesake of a minelayer) and *Fort Worden* (honours the namesake of DD *Worden*, removed after review) were left out.
- **CV (Essex-class convention, battles):** 48 US victories or defining stands (Revolution, Barbary War, Tippecanoe, War of 1812, Texas and Mexican wars, Civil War Union victories, 1898, 1918, 1942–45). Battles already used by an escort carrier (*Guadalcanal*, *Saipan*, *Okinawa*, *Salerno Bay*, ...) or covered by an existing CV name (*Leyte Gulf* within *Leyte*, *Plattsburgh* within *Lake Champlain*) were skipped. Several entries also sit in the `USA_BATTLES` theme pool, which is allowed (thematic pools are an alternative pick, not a hull).

## Kept on judgment
- `CrossClassPerson CA/CV : Lincoln ~ Abraham Lincoln`: the capital of Nebraska, named after the president; a place named after a person stays (`CLAUDE.md` §5 triage). The Navy has carried both *Lincoln* and *Abraham Lincoln*.
- `Columbia ~ District of Columbia`, `Washington ~ George Washington`, `Winston-Salem ~ Salem`: kept from the 2026-10-10 authoring, unchanged.
- CA *Dallas* also appears in `USA_DD_HISTORICAL` (USS *Dallas*, DD-199, named for Alexander J. Dallas). Kept: it is the only addition with a real cruiser record (the canceled Des Moines-class hull), and DD sits outside the cross-class rule. *Madison*, *Stockton* and *Lawrence* were dropped for the same overlap because they had no such record.
- BB *James Madison* beside DD *Madison*: different persons (DD-425 honours Cdr. James J. Madison, see Research), so not a same-person pair.
