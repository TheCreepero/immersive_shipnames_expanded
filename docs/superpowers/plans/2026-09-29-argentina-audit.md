## Rationale
- **CrossClass**:
  - Historic names go to the class that carried them: Veinticinco de Mayo and Independencia to CV (Colossus-class carriers), Libertad to CA (1890s coastal battleship), Nueve de Julio to CL (protected cruiser, then ex-USS Boise).
  - Almirante Brown stays in CA (1929 heavy cruiser). República, Constitución and República Argentina stay in BB as sovereignty symbols.
  - Quilmes and San Lorenzo stay in BC (battles) and leave the CL city list.
- **CA / BB split**: CA holds independence-era and civil-war generals and caudillos, and BB holds civilian founders, presidents and compacts. Ten persons were removed from CA. Mitre, Sarmiento, Urquiza, Saavedra, Moreno, Rivadavia, Castelli and Monteagudo stay in BB. The full-name forms General San Martín and Manuel Belgrano were removed, and the persons stay in CA under the historic armored-cruiser names San Martín and General Belgrano.
- **CrossClassPerson**:
  - The historic Garibaldi-class armored cruiser names (San Martín, General Belgrano, Pueyrredón) stay in CA, so their full-name forms leave BB.
  - The BB historic dreadnoughts Rivadavia and Moreno stay, so the full-name duplicates leave BB.
  - Presidente Sarmiento (a sail training frigate, not a cruiser) moves from CL to a new DD 'Historic Training Ships' section, so BB keeps Domingo Faustino Sarmiento.
- **Depth top-up**:
  - CA 31 -> 37 with six independence-era generals.
  - BB 30 -> 36 with four founders/presidents and two constitutional compacts (Pacto Federal 1831, Acuerdo de San Nicolás 1852).
  - CV 28 -> 33 with the Zonda wind and four Andean summits (Aconcagua, Lanín, Tronador, Chaltén).
- **BC / BATTLES**:
  - Six unattested names were replaced with attested engagements: Monte Santiago (1827), El Buceo (1814), Costa Brava (1842), Arroyo de la China (1814, naval), Callao (1816, Brown's Pacific cruise) and Yavi (1816).
  - San Nicolás (1811) and Ensenada (1827) were attested but skipped because they are CL city names.
- **Respellings**:
  - DD: Indomable -> Indómita, Zapiola -> Comandante General Zapiola, Cordero -> Contraalmirante Cordero (the historical ship forms).
  - SS: Renquecurá -> Reuquecurá.
- **Review follow-up (DD)**:
  - Céspedes was dropped (not in any Gurruchaga or Brown fleet list).
  - Comodoro Lasserre -> Comodoro Augusto Lasserre (the ship's official form).
  - Cazador, Cometa, Flecha, Atrevido, Vengador, Triunfo, Vanguardia and Corsario moved from the 'Historic Torpedo Boats' section to the 'Martial Descriptors' section. As martial nouns they are plausible, but they are not attested as historic hulls.
- **Section headers**: BB 'Historic Dreadnoughts & Capital Ship Program Proposals' -> 'Historic Dreadnoughts' (Roca moved to the founders section). CV birds section -> 'Native Birds & Raptors'.

## Kept on judgment
- Depth PROVINCES (23/35): the user accepted the scope (Argentina has 23 provinces).
- CrossClassPerson CL/CA Necochea ~ Mariano Necochea, General Lavalle ~ Juan Lavalle: CL entries are cities named after the persons.
- CrossClassPerson CL/BB Comodoro Rivadavia ~ Rivadavia, Rawson ~ Guillermo Rawson, San Nicolás ~ Acuerdo de San Nicolás: CL entries are cities (the Acuerdo is a compact named after the city).
- BC doctrine: the group is built on battle honours, both land and naval, following Argentine ship-naming practice (ARA Chacabuco, Maipú). It is decoupled from BB, so it was kept.
- DD King: an authentic ship name (ARA King) that is also an English word. Kept as the documented historical form.
- DD/HEROES Py: an authentic ship name (Bouchard-class minesweeper Py) and distinct from the Comodoro Py hulls, so both are kept.
- DD 'Martial Descriptors' nouns (Tenaz, Centinela, Vigilante, Temerario and similar): not persons. They are thematic extrapolation for wartime flotillas (CLAUDE.md §3 plausibility), and none is presented as a historic hull.
- BC_HISTORICAL and BATTLES share the same 35 battle names: this is deliberate. BC binds battle honours to the hull, and BATTLES offers the same theme to any hull in the Ship Designer.
