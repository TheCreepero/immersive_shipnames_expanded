## Rationale
- **BB/CV**: Minas Gerais, São Paulo, Independência and Guanabara stay in BB (the 1910 dreadnoughts; states and sovereignty symbols). CV drops them and also drops Paranaguá, which duplicated Baía de Paranaguá. CV's Todos os Santos → Baía de Todos os Santos (matches the other bays). New CV names:
  - Sirius, Canopus: 1957 Brazilian Navy hydrographic ships.
  - Antares: Brazilian Navy oceanographic ship; also a star on the national flag.
  - Baía de Marajó, Baía da Ilha Grande.
- **CL/BB**: Bahia and Rio Grande do Sul stay in CL (the 1910 scout cruisers). Rio de Janeiro stays in BB (the dreadnought, later HMS Agincourt).
- **CA/BB**: Tiradentes and Sete de Setembro go to BB (founding father, Independence Day). Duque de Caxias stays in CA with the other marshals. BB gains Rui Barbosa (author of the 1891 constitution) and Ipiranga (the Cry of Ipiranga).
- **BB/BC and BC doctrine (§2)**: the naval battles leave BB, and BC is rebuilt around decisive naval encounters and river passages plus coastal fortresses. It keeps 9 naval entries (Riachuelo, Passo da Pátria, Humaitá, Curupaiti, Itapiru, Curuzu, Tonelero, Angostura, Cuevas) plus Fortaleza de Santa Cruz, and adds:
  - Cisplatine War, Independence and Platine actions: Quatro de Maio, Monte Santiago, Paissandu.
  - Paraguayan War passages: Mercedes, Timbó.
  - 16 more coastal fortresses (17 with Santa Cruz, 31 in total): São João, Copacabana, Itaipu, Laje, Reis Magos, São Marcelo, Santo Antônio da Barra, Monte Serrat, Brum, Orange, Presépio, Macapá, Príncipe da Beira, Anhatomirim, Ponta Grossa, Barra Grande.

  The land battles stay in the universal BATTLES pool. Laguna (the 1867 retreat) goes to CL (the port). The "historic war vessels / flagships" clause has no entries: Brazil's flagships (Amazonas, Niterói, Brasil, Pedro I) already sit in BB, CL or CV, and Brazil has no pre-colonial thalassocracy.
- **BATTLES top-up**: the same naval actions as BC (Quatro de Maio, Monte Santiago, Paissandu, Mercedes, Timbó), Paraguayan War battles (Estero Bellaco, Boqueirão, Lomas Valentinas), Uruçumirim (1567, Guanabara), and the FEB campaign in Italy (Camaiore, Collecchio, Fornovo).
- **CA duplicates**: Barão do Amazonas (= Almirante Barroso) and Visconde de Inhaúma (= Almirante Inhaúma) removed. Almirante Cochrane → Marquês do Maranhão, his Brazilian title, which avoids the Chilean ship name. Almirante Grenfell and Almirante Taylor added (vice-admirals of the Independence navy).
- **Respellings (BATTLES/BC)**: Coruzu → Curuzu; Tuiutí → Tuiuti; Toneleros → Tonelero; Montevideo → Montevidéu; Colonia do Sacramento → Colônia do Sacramento. Itapiru keeps its modern unaccented form; Piribebuy keeps the pt.wikipedia list form.
- **STATES**: historical units added: Cisplatina and Grão-Pará (imperial provinces); Guanabara (state 1960–75); Iguaçu, Ponta Porã, Fernando de Noronha, Guaporé and Rio Branco (federal territories of 1943); Itamaracá (hereditary captaincy). Display name → "States & Territories" (the reviewer's suggestion; most additions are the 1943 federal territories).
- **HEROES**: added Maria Quitéria, Joana Angélica and Zumbi dos Palmares (all in the Livro dos Heróis e Heroínas da Pátria).
- **INDIGENOUS**: display name → "Native Peoples & Chiefs", because the pool holds five named caciques.
