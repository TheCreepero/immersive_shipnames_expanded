# Finland (FIN) Ship Namelists

Source file: `common/units/names_ships/FIN_ship_names.txt`

---

## Overview

Finland's historical naval forces in World War II revolved around coastal defense ships (*Väinämöinen*, *Ilmarinen*), submarines (*Vetehinen*, *Vesihiisi*, *Iku-Turso*, *Saukko*, *Vesikko*), gunboats, motor torpedo boats, and minelayers. In vanilla Hearts of Iron IV, Finnish ship namelists are sparse and quickly run dry during Baltic naval expansion campaigns. ISNE provides expansive, historically grounded namelists for both historical coastal defense operations and plausible great-power Baltic expansions across 7 dedicated ship-type classes and 13 universal thematic topic pools.
