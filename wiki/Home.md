## Design Philosophy

ISNE prioritizes **historical plausibility over rigid accuracy**. Names are anchored in authentic naval doctrines, ship class conventions, peacetime construction plans, and cultural/mythological traditions — then extrapolated to support full wartime campaigns, expanded navies, and alternate-history naval buildups.

Key features:
- **Linguistic precision** — native-language diacritics, proper grammatical cases, and authentic spelling throughout
- **Gameplay scalability** — dozens of unique entries per hull type with formatted fallback names
- **Coverage breadth** — destroyers, torpedo boats, submarines, light cruisers, heavy cruisers, battleships, battlecruisers, and carriers
